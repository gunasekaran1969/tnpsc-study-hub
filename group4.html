📋 Group 4 Exam Details

🎯 What is TNPSC Group 4?

TNPSC Group 4 is a Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission recruitment examination for various Group 4 services. Candidates should always refer to the latest official notification for the exact posts, vacancies, eligibility, dates and examination details.

📝 General Examination Areas

  • Tamil / English language section as specified in the latest notification
  • General Studies
  • Aptitude and Mental Ability
  • Current Affairs
  • Tamil Nadu related topics

⚠️ Important

The exact examination pattern, number of questions, marks, duration, qualifying requirements and syllabus can change between recruitment notifications. Always verify the latest details from the official TNPSC notification before applying.

📝 Examination Pattern

Area What to Prepare
🔤 Language Tamil / English preparation according to the applicable notification
🌏 General Studies History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Science and related topics
🔢 Aptitude Mathematics, numerical ability and mental ability
📰 Current Affairs Tamil Nadu, India, world events, government schemes, awards, sports and important developments
