🏆 Group 1 – 25 Questions

General preparation questions for Group 1 aspirants.

1. Which body conducts the TNPSC examinations?
Answer: Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC)
2. What is the capital of Tamil Nadu?
Answer: Chennai
3. Which is the highest court in Tamil Nadu?
Answer: Madras High Court
4. Who is the constitutional head of a state?
Answer: Governor
5. Who is the executive head of the state government?
Answer: Chief Minister
6. What is the supreme law of India?
Answer: Constitution of India
7. How many Houses does the Tamil Nadu Legislature have?
Answer: One
8. What is the minimum voting age in India?
Answer: 18 years
9. Which institution is known as the guardian of the Constitution?
Answer: Supreme Court of India
10. Which fundamental right deals with equality?
Answer: Right to Equality
11. What does GDP stand for?
Answer: Gross Domestic Product
12. Which sector includes agriculture?
Answer: Primary sector
13. Which is the longest river in Tamil Nadu?
Answer: Kaveri
14. Which sea lies to the east of Tamil Nadu?
Answer: Bay of Bengal
15. What is the study of earthquakes called?
Answer: Seismology
16. Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?
Answer: Nitrogen
17. Who is associated with the discovery of gravity?
Answer: Isaac Newton
18. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
Answer: Mars
19. What is India's national animal?
Answer: Bengal Tiger
20. What is India's national flower?
Answer: Lotus
21. What is the national currency of India?
Answer: Indian Rupee
22. Which movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in 1942?
Answer: Quit India Movement
23. Who wrote the national anthem of India?
Answer: Rabindranath Tagore
24. Which ancient Tamil work is attributed to Thiruvalluvar?
Answer: Thirukkural
25. Which organisation is responsible for India's monetary policy?
Answer: Reserve Bank of India
