🧠 Group 4 Practice Test

Answer all questions and press Submit Practice Test. Explanations will appear after submission.

1. What is the capital of Tamil Nadu?

Answer: Chennai. Chennai is the capital city of Tamil Nadu.

2. Which is the national animal of India?

Answer: Tiger. The Bengal tiger is India's national animal.

3. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

Answer: Mars. Mars is commonly called the Red Planet.

4. How many days are there in a leap year?

Answer: 366 days. A leap year contains 366 days.

5. Which organ pumps blood throughout the human body?

Answer: Heart. The heart pumps blood through the circulatory system.
