📝 How to Apply for a TNPSC Examination

  • Visit the official TNPSC website.
  • Check the relevant examination notification.
  • Read the eligibility requirements carefully.
  • Complete One Time Registration (OTR), if required.
  • Log in and apply for the particular examination.
  • Upload the required documents and photograph/signature according to the official instructions.
  • Complete the required fee payment, if applicable.
  • Save your application details for future reference.

🔐 Important: One Time Registration

TNPSC's official instructions state that One Time Registration and the applicant dashboard are mandatory before applying through the online system. OTR itself is not an application for a particular examination; candidates must apply separately for the examination they wish to take.
