🏆 Group 1 Exam Details

📋 About Group 1

TNPSC Group 1 is a competitive examination for various higher-level services in Tamil Nadu. The exact posts, vacancies, eligibility, examination stages and selection procedure depend on the official recruitment notification.

📚 Group 1 Preparation Areas

  • General Studies
  • Indian History and Culture
  • Indian Polity and Governance
  • Indian Economy
  • Indian and Tamil Nadu Geography
  • Science and Technology
  • Environment and Ecology
  • Tamil Nadu administration and development
  • Current Affairs

🌅 Morning – General Studies

Study one major topic such as History, Polity, Geography or Economy.

☀️ Afternoon – Language

Practise Tamil / applicable language topics, grammar and comprehension.

🌆 Evening – Aptitude

Solve numerical ability and reasoning questions.

Revise important Tamil Nadu, national and international developments.

Take a timed test, review mistakes and focus on weak areas.
